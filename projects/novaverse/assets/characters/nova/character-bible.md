# NOVA — OFFICIAL CHARACTER BIBLE

Project: NovaVerse
Character name: Nova
Status: Official character identity

## Core Identity
Nova is the recurring presenter and main character of NovaVerse.
She is an adult-looking, stylish, adventurous and family-friendly
3D animated female character.

## Visual Identity
- Blonde hair.
- Expressive, attractive and friendly face.
- Soft fantasy 3D visual style.
- Modern, stylish and adventurous appearance.
- Black leather jacket.
- Black leather skirt.
- Tall, glossy black boots.
- Consistent facial features, hairstyle and outfit across videos.

## Personality
- Curious
- Intelligent
- Witty
- Brave
- Kind
- Confident
- Energetic
- Adventurous

## Acting and Expression
Nova should use expressive facial animation and natural body language.
Her reactions should be clear, entertaining and suitable for science
storytelling.

Typical expressions:
- Curiosity
- Surprise
- Excitement
- Amusement
- Thoughtfulness
- Friendly confidence

## Animation Rules
- Preserve the same character identity in every episode.
- Do not change her hair color or signature outfit.
- Do not replace her with a robot, alien or unrelated character.
- Avoid sexualized poses or presentation.
- Prefer natural gestures and readable expressions.
- Keep animation family-friendly.

## Visual Direction
Soft fantasy 3D.
Polished cinematic lighting.
Expressive animation.
Modern science-adventure atmosphere.

## Production Notes
This document defines the official character identity.
It is not a 3D model or a rigged character file.

Reference images, model files, textures and rig files must be
stored in this same character directory when available.

Do not claim that a 3D model exists unless the actual model files
have been verified.
